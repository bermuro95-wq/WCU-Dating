Welcome to **WCU Dating**—a premium, secure, and student-exclusive social and relationship platform custom-built for the vibrant community of **Wachemo University**. Designed from the ground up to foster genuine friendships, study partnerships, and meaningful connections within a safe campus ecosystem.

---

## 🌟 The Story Behind the Project

Navigating campus life at Wachemo University is an unforgettable journey, but finding kindred spirits, study partners, or someone special across different departments shouldn't feel like searching for a needle in a haystack. 

**WCU Dating** was born out of a simple desire: to bridge the campus social gap with technology that feels warm, personal, and secure. Built with a modern aesthetic, real-time messaging, and strict student-centric validation, it gives WCU students a cozy digital home to connect, chat, and build memories that last a lifetime.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Experience

* **🔒 Student-Exclusive Ecosystem:** Designed specifically for WCU students with structured department filters, year tracking, and profile customization.
* **⚡ Real-Time Facebook-Style Messaging:** Instant messaging powered by Firebase Firestore, complete with live online status indicators, unread badges, typing/delivery cues, and message management.
* **🛡️ Advanced Safety & Privacy Controls:** Built-in user blocking, report mechanisms, chat clearing, and conversation management so your peace of mind is always protected[cite: 13].
* **🔍 Smart Discovery & Filters:** Filter peers dynamically by department, study year, or search directly by name to find your ideal study buddy or friend[cite: 13].
* **🎨 Modern Glassmorphism & Matrix Aesthetics:** A gorgeous, romantic UI palette featuring smooth animations, responsive mobile design, and dedicated dark-mode accents[cite: 13].

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3 (Custom animations, glassmorphism, responsive flex/grid layouts), Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)[cite: 13].
* **Backend & Cloud:** Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore (Real-time NoSQL database), Firebase Storage (Profile photo uploads)[cite: 13].
* **UI Libraries:** Font Awesome 6.4.2 icons, Google Fonts (Inter & Poppins)[cite: 13].
